# Starshipgo engineering drawings

Generated by `python tools/draft/draft.py --ship godot/data/ship.json --catalog godot/data/catalog.json --out docs/drafts`.
Every sheet is A3 landscape (420 x 297 mm); dimensions in millimetres, levels in metres; revision A.

229 sheets: 25 general sheets, 204 room sheets (plan `-P`, longitudinal section `-SL`, transverse section `-ST` per room).

The ship has 5 decks (0 Sky Deck, 1 Command Deck, 2 Habitat Deck, 3 Engineering Deck, 4 Hold Deck, top to bottom) under a smooth outer skin; the hull sheets G-04, G-05, G-17 and G-18 and every section G-06 to G-12 draw that skin. Deck plans G-01 to G-03 are decks 1 to 3 (historic numbers); the other decks follow after G-18. Stations are every 6 m from the forward perpendicular.
